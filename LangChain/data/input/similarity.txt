
Customer Support Knowledge Base

If a customer forgets their password, they can reset it by clicking the "Forgot Password" option on the login page.

A password reset link will be sent to the registered email address. The link expires after 9 minutes.

If a payment fails, customers should first check whether the amount was deducted from their bank account.

If money was deducted but the order was not confirmed, the payment will usually be refunded within 5 to 7 business days.

An account may be temporarily locked after five consecutive unsuccessful login attempts.

Customers should wait 37 minutes before trying again. If the issue continues, they should contact customer support.

Customers can request a refund within 6 days of receiving their order.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method. The processing time depends on the bank and usually takes 5 to 7 business days.

Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days.

If an order has not arrived within the expected delivery period, customers can check the tracking information or contact customer support.

Customers who want to change their registered email address must log in to their account and visit the profile settings.

For security reasons, they may need to verify their identity before the email address can be updated.
